ⓘ Front door open · 6 min

One drum, shared by the machines that are on: whole, halves on the diagonal, or thirds. A combined washer-dryer takes the washer's place and turns it off with the dryer. Paused freezes the motion; done settles the load and fills the arc; error marks the arc and text. Time left comes from progress here; the glance reads it from the machine.
